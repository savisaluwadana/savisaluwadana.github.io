Agentic systems I’m interested in

I’m interested in agents that use tools, retrieval and workflow state to help people complete real business and developer tasks. I want to explore systems where the model’s reasoning connects to reliable APIs, explicit permissions, useful evaluation and observable execution.

  • Tool-using agents and MCP interfaces that respect domain rules and the permissions of the person using them.
  • Durable workflow orchestration, retries and recovery for tasks that span multiple services or take time to complete.
  • Multi-agent collaboration with clear responsibilities, shared evidence and human review for consequential actions.
  • Retrieval-augmented generation, evidence provenance and agent evaluation that make results inspectable and useful.

My current product work includes Pain Intelligence Lab, Property Management OS and DevRelOS. Follow their requests and state changes in the agentic systems architecture walkthroughs. The broader topics above describe my development interests.
