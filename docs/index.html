03 / Approach

Good engineering should feel calm.

My preference is for systems that are understandable under pressure: fewer surprises, clear interfaces and enough observability to know what is actually happening.

  1. 01
    Make the system legible.

    Clear boundaries, visible state and fewer hidden assumptions.

  2. 02
    Automate repetition, not judgment.

    Use automation to remove toil while keeping important decisions inspectable.

  3. 03
    Design for operations.

    Shipping is only the start. Software should be observable, debuggable and recoverable.

  4. 04
    Treat internal tooling as product work.

    Developers are users too. Good platform engineering respects their time.
